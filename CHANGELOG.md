## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Crafting recipes for copper, iron, golden and diamond horse armor and nautilus armor.
- Enchanting of horse and nautilus armor at the enchanting table and the anvil, limited to a
  configurable whitelist per armor type.
- Optional durability for mount armor, with Unbreaking and Mending.
- Optional armorer trades for horse and nautilus armor.
- Optional client part that follows the server's item settings.
